## Market-structure story: BEI ends stock suspensions and issuer-wide UMA
- At a joint BEI–OJK press conference on 2026-10-26, BEI President Director Jeffrey Hendrik said BEI will **stop suspending stocks and stop issuer-wide UMA notices**. Instead it will freeze or block the brokerage accounts under suspicion and announce investigations, naming the trading period and the brokers. Brokers are being briefed this week. Start: "early November 2026" (Suara) or "very soon" (InvestorTrust). BEI also said its backtests show the HSC exclusion on 2026-11-02 will not cause a sharp IHSG drop.
- OJK (Hasan Fawzi): the shareholder disclosure threshold drops from >5% to **>1%**, and investor categories go from 9 to 39.
- Sources: Suara, "IHSG Bebas Jual-Beli! BEI Resmi Cabut Aturan Suspend Saham, Begini Skema Barunya", 12:10 WIB; InvestorTrust, "BEI Kurangi UMA, Pengawasan Saham Beralih ke Investigasi Dugaan Manipulasi Harga", 13:12 WIB. Bisnis, "BEI Hapus Suspensi Perdagangan Saham Genjot Likuiditas" is headline-only. The primary BEI announcement was not read.
- **Desk impact:** the CLAUDE.md rule "suspended, full call auction and UMA stocks get no tickets" relies on those notices. Once they stop, stocks under investigation keep trading with no board signal. The morning desk should consider a new check: an investigation notice naming a stock is a BOARD flag. `tools/idx.py board` already infers UMA from issuers' replies, so that inference will go quiet. Proposed for the desk head; the night shift does not change desk rules.
